TailWatcher

A free, open-source app for your Start9 server that sends a phone alert when the planes or flights you watch take off and land.

Coming soon to the Start9 community store
  • Watch a tail number or a flightFollow a family plane by its N-number, or an airline flight like UA1234.
  • Alerts through ntfyTakeoff (and optionally landing) notifications on your phone, with a link to the live track on ADS-B Exchange.
  • Runs on your own serverA StartOS app that uses free ADS-B feeds. No account and no subscription.
